Projects

Computer Science Term 2, 2026

Library book tracker

A small desktop program that keeps track of which books our class library has lent out and warns when one is overdue. Built in Python with a Tkinter window and a plain text file for storage, so it runs on any school computer without installing anything.

What I learned: writing to a file is easy, but deciding what happens when two people edit it at once is the hard part.

Science Term 1, 2026

Air quality readings for our street

For the science fair I logged dust levels outside the school gate every morning for six weeks and charted the results against traffic counts. The chart is drawn from the raw numbers in a spreadsheet, and the poster explains the method so another student can repeat it.

What I learned: six weeks of data is enough to see a pattern and nowhere near enough to prove a cause.

Design Term 3, 2025

School notice board website

Our notices used to be taped to a wall and half of them fell off. I rebuilt them as a single page that teachers can update, with the newest notice at the top and a printable version for the wall anyway. Plain HTML and CSS, no framework.

What I learned: the people using it care far more about how fast it loads than how it looks.

Mathematics Term 2, 2025

Prime number visualiser

A page that draws primes in a spiral so you can see the diagonal lines they fall into. Made to go with a class presentation on why primes thin out as numbers grow.

What I learned: a picture explained the idea to my class in ten seconds, after two slides of notation had failed.
